## 🔐 Password Manager

A new page in the sidebar for everything you sign in to.

- **Encrypted before anything is saved or synced.** Your master password never leaves the PC, and every login is sealed on its own. Titles, user names and websites are encrypted too, so your account only ever holds data it cannot read.
- **Everything in one place:**
  - search, favorites and folders;
  - a warning for weak and reused passwords;
  - every earlier password of a login kept in its history;
  - a generator for strong passwords.
- **Synced with your account.** Sign in on another PC and your vault is there, opened with the same master password. A login changed on two PCs keeps the newer change, and the other password goes into its history, so none is lost.
- **Recovery code.** Shown once when you create the vault. It is the only way back in if you forget the master password, so keep it somewhere safe.
- **Windows Hello.** Turn it on from the ⋯ menu. When you open the page, Windows Hello asks for your PIN, fingerprint or face right away.
- **Locks by itself:**
  - after the idle time you choose;
  - when Windows locks or the PC wakes from sleep.
- **Safe copying.** A copied password stays out of Windows' clipboard history and cloud clipboard, and is cleared after 30 seconds. A password you show hides itself again after 30 seconds.
- **Import and export.** Bring your passwords from a Chrome, Edge or Firefox export, Bitwarden or 1Password. Export an encrypted backup under its own password; the export asks for your master password first.
- **Website icons.** Every login shows its site's icon. MYLE fetches it from the site itself, not from an icon service, and keeps it encrypted on your PC.
- **Fits any window,** from a small one to a 4K screen. On a narrow window the list and the login take turns.

## 🧩 Browser filling

MYLE Passwords, an extension for Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox. Add it from **Password Manager → ⋯ → Browser filling**, which shows its folder and the steps, until it is in the browser stores.

- **Click a sign-in field** to see your logins for that site, with their icons. On a password step that already names your account, as Google does, that account comes first.
- **Suggest a strong password** on sign-up and password-change forms.
- **Save and update.** After you sign in with a new login or a changed password, it offers to save it.
- **The toolbar button** lists the logins for the page and its sign-in frames, with search.
- **Built so a web page cannot steal your passwords:**
  - It fills only when you click, only on https pages, and only with the logins saved for that very site. Look-alike addresses get nothing.
  - Its menu is out of the page's reach. A click counts only when it lands on the menu itself, not through something drawn over it.
  - It never fills a hidden field or a form that would send your password unencrypted.
  - A sign-in form embedded from another site is filled only from the toolbar button, where you can see whose it is.

## ⌨️ Windows programs and the tray

- **Ctrl+Shift+L** in a program's sign-in window lists the logins linked to that program, and fills the one you choose.
- **MYLE stays in the tray** next to the clock once you have a vault, so filling keeps working with the window closed. You can switch this off in Settings.

## 🛡️ Hardened

- **Wrong master passwords slow down:** each try after the third waits longer.
- **Your account cannot add or delete your logins.** Every entry, and every deletion, must be sealed with your vault's key. Anything else is refused and repaired from your PC. Nothing from the account is taken in while the vault is locked.
- **A damaged entry never locks you out.** The rest of the vault opens, and the page tells you how many entries could not be opened.
- **Browser requests are limited,** and they never keep an unattended vault open.
